background. Only the final sentence is an ask.

The product has existed for three years. The original team was small, the data
model has grown organically, and every quarter someone has proposed replacing the
navigation. The current colors are old but recognizable. The typography was
chosen before the company had a design system. There are many possible ways to
organize the information architecture. Some people prefer tabs, some prefer a
sidebar, and some prefer a single scrolling page. The analytics team believes
the funnel is healthy, while sales believes it is confusing. We have discussed
this in several meetings. Nobody has committed to a redesign. The old logo is
still used in email signatures. There is a long history of customer requests
about search, filters, and dates. Some are contradictory. The support queue has
a mixture of bug reports and requests. The team is busy. There may be a better
way to present the data. Nobody has agreed on what that way is.

The one concrete request is: **add a keyboard shortcut for focusing the filter
box, and show the shortcut in the control's tooltip**.
